I am currently pursuing the professional Ph.D. degree in Communication Engineering at [Shenzhen University](https://www.szu.edu.cn/), under the supervision of [Prof. Yongsheng Liang（梁永生教授）](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=aOEk854AAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I received the M.Eng. degree in Software Engineering from [Dalian Jiaotong University](https://www.djtu.edu.cn/) in 2025 and the B.Eng. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Zhengzhou Technology University in 2022.

My research focuses on **6G-oriented video semantic transmission**, especially semantic representation, generative reconstruction, and adaptive transmission under complex channels and bandwidth constraints. My broader interests include semantic communication, deep joint source-channel coding, computer vision, video restoration, and object detection.
